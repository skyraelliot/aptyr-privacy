# Aptyr Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Provider: Skyra Elliot

Contact: [support@aptyr.com](mailto:support@aptyr.com)

## About Aptyr

Aptyr helps you plan and record workouts around your schedule, available equipment, and recovery. This policy describes the current beta app.

Aptyr is provided by Skyra Elliot, referred to in this policy as the developer or “we.” For privacy questions, contact [support@aptyr.com](mailto:support@aptyr.com).

Aptyr has no user account or cloud training backend. Your profile, training, workout history, shifts, and active session records are stored locally on your device. Aptyr does not automatically upload these records to developer infrastructure. Device backup and transfer services are separate from Aptyr's storage and may retain or restore copies depending on your platform and settings.

## Information stored on your device

Aptyr uses the following information to personalize workout recommendations, resume workouts, and show your progress:

- **Setup and preferences:** your selected training program, weekly training availability, usual workout duration, Home or Gym choice, home equipment, weight units, and whether you work shifts. Home or Gym is a choice you make, not your geographic location.
- **Daily check-ins and adjustments:** your daily status, energy, available time, equipment, recovery information, sleep information, and any adjustment reasons or notes you provide. Some choices have default values. Daily check-ins can be skipped, and sleep hours are optional.
- **Shift Log:** dates, work status, shift timing, overtime, on-call and travel/camp choices, and optional shift times and notes. Saving a daily check-in also creates or updates that day's shift context.
- **Workouts and progress:** workout dates and durations, exercises, completion status, substitutions, optional repetitions, weights and notes, personal records, and progress through your program.
- **Workouts in progress:** the workout plan, completed and skipped exercises, logs, timer progress, and timing information needed to resume a session.
- **Earlier beta feedback:** if you used an older beta, feedback saved on your device may remain there. The current app does not send those records to us.

Setup choices are needed to complete onboarding. Shift entries, detailed exercise logs, notes, and external feedback are optional. Recommendations and progress summaries are calculated on your device.

The app may temporarily keep a small record of feature events, such as starting or completing a workout. Each event contains only an event name and timestamp. This list stays in memory and ends with the app process; it is not sent to remote analytics. Reset local data does not immediately clear this temporary list. Aptyr does not use a remote analytics SDK or a remote crash-reporting system.

## Information you choose to send

You can choose to contact support or complete the beta survey. These actions use services outside Aptyr. The app does not attach your profile, workout history, shift entries, or saved notes to either action. Information you add yourself is handled through the service you choose.

## Beta survey

Tapping **Complete Beta Survey** opens an external [Google Form](https://forms.gle/aM1etz31g3JFkuUb8) through your device's link handler, normally a browser. Aptyr does not fill in or submit answers, pass your saved training data to the form, or track whether you complete it.

Google handles the form once you open it. Review the information shown on the form and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) before responding. Participation is optional.

The survey does not require your name or email address. You may voluntarily provide information about your work schedule, device, workouts, and experience using Aptyr. Responses are accessible to the Aptyr developer and are used to evaluate the beta experience, identify usability and product issues, and improve Aptyr. Responses are not guaranteed to be anonymous. Google may independently process information under its own policies.

## Support communications

Tapping **Email Support** opens a draft addressed to **support@aptyr.com** in your email app. The subject is “Aptyr support.” The draft includes the app version, platform (iOS or Android), build number, and build profile. It does not include a device identifier, device model, operating-system version, or your saved training records.

You can edit the draft and choose whether to send it. If you send it, the receiving mailbox receives your email address, message, and anything else you include, such as your name or attachments. Your email provider and the receiving Gmail service handle the communication under their own terms and privacy policies.

Support communications are accessible to the Aptyr developer and are used to respond to support requests, investigate reported problems, and improve Aptyr. Aptyr does not automatically attach your profile, workout history, shift records, or notes.

## Third-party platforms and services

Aptyr is built with Expo and React Native and uses local device features for storage, display, audio playback, and vibration. It does not use an Expo cloud database or an in-app account service.

If you install or test Aptyr through Google Play or TestFlight, those platforms handle their own distribution and testing services. The iOS beta directs testers to submit bug reports in TestFlight. Aptyr itself does not send a TestFlight report or attach screenshots. Information you provide through a store, testing platform, browser, or email service is separate from the app's local records and is subject to the relevant service's policies.

## Permissions

Aptyr plays bundled timer sounds and may vibrate or keep the screen awake during a workout. It does not record audio or request microphone access.

The current app has no camera, photo-library, or geographic-location feature, does not access your contacts, and has no HealthKit or Health Connect integration. It does not request notification or cross-app tracking permission, or use advertising identifiers. Health and fitness information that you enter yourself, such as sleep or exercise logs, remains part of your local app records.

## Data sharing, advertising, and tracking

The current Aptyr app does not sell your local personal information, share it for advertising, display ads, or profile you for advertising. It contains no advertising SDK or cross-app tracking feature. Optional support and survey communications are described above.

## Data retention and deletion

Saved app records remain on your device until you remove or replace them, reset the app, or remove its local storage. There is no fixed automatic deletion schedule for workout history or older local feedback.

To clear saved Aptyr records, go to **Settings → Reset local data → Confirm reset**. When successful, this removes Aptyr's saved application records from its current local app-data store: your profile, preferences, workout history, active workout, shifts, check-in, adjustments, and any older locally saved feedback. The app returns to onboarding. This is not a guarantee of secure erasure. Temporary feature events end when the app process ends; resetting saved data does not immediately clear that temporary list.

Uninstalling the app normally removes its local app storage, but does not guarantee forensic deletion. Device backups or transfers may retain or restore copies depending on your platform and settings. Resetting or uninstalling Aptyr does not delete those copies or information you already sent elsewhere. Manage those copies through the relevant platform or service.

Aptyr has no account or training database on a developer-operated server to delete. Support messages, submitted survey responses, and platform beta feedback are separate: resetting the app does not delete them.

Survey responses are retained while reasonably needed to evaluate the beta, prepare Aptyr for launch, investigate product issues, and improve the app, then may be deleted when no longer reasonably needed.

Support communications are retained while reasonably needed to respond to the request, investigate the issue, and provide reasonable follow-up, then may be deleted when no longer reasonably needed.

TestFlight and Google Play beta feedback may be retained as reasonably needed for beta testing, troubleshooting, and product development, subject also to the relevant platform's own retention and practices. There is no fixed deletion schedule for these external communications.

If information held by the developer can reasonably be linked to you, you may contact **support@aptyr.com** to request its deletion. Some anonymous or otherwise unidentifiable survey responses may not be capable of being linked back to an individual and therefore may not be identifiable for deletion. A request to the developer does not delete copies controlled independently by Google, Apple, your email provider, device backups, or other services.

## Security

Aptyr keeps training records in the app's local storage and does not upload them to a training backend. Access to your device and its backups can affect the privacy of those records. Protect your device with its available security controls. No method of storing or transmitting information can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Children's privacy

The Aptyr beta is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through the beta. The current beta does not ask for an age or date of birth and does not include an age-verification step.
